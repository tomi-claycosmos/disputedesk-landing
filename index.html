How it will work

1 · Connect

Install the Shopify app

New Shopify Payments disputes come in on their own. Stripe and PayPal are planned.

2 · Draft

AI drafts the evidence

AI reads the dispute reason, then pulls your order, shipping and tracking, customer messages and policies into one PDF, in date order, with a draft response. It does the busywork. It never submits anything.

3 · Review

You decide

Check what the AI wrote, edit it, approve it, submit it. Nothing goes out until you click. You get reminders before the deadline.

FAQ

Will I win?

I can't promise that. The card network and the bank decide. The aim is to help you send complete evidence on time.

Will anything be sent without my approval?

No. Every packet waits for you to review it and click submit.

Which payment providers?

Shopify Payments first. Stripe and PayPal after that.

What data does it access?

Only what the evidence needs: dispute, order, shipping and tracking data, plus helpdesk messages if you connect them. You can uninstall at any time.

Pre-dispute alerts?

Not at first. If you need them, tell me when you join.

Why no commission?

Building a packet costs about the same for a $40 dispute as for a $4,000 one. A flat price seems fairer.
