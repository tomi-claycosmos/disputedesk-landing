FAQ

Do you guarantee I'll win?

No. The card network and the issuing bank decide the outcome. We help you send complete, well-organized evidence on time.

Will anything be submitted without my approval?

No. By default every packet waits for you to review it and click submit.

Which payment providers are supported?

Shopify Payments at launch. Stripe and PayPal are planned next.

What data do you access?

Only what the evidence needs: dispute, order, fulfillment and tracking data, plus helpdesk conversations if you connect them. You can uninstall at any time.

Do you offer pre-dispute alerts?

Not at launch. If alerts matter to you, tell us when you join the waitlist.

Why no commission?

Building a packet costs us about the same for a $40 dispute as for a $4,000 one, so we think a flat price is fairer.
